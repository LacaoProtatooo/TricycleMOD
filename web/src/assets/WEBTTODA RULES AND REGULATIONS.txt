WEBTTODA RULES AND REGULATIONS

The following infraction and their corresponding penalties are prescribed to maintain the members highest work efficiency, smooth and successful operation of the association and help the WEBTTODA contribute its effectiveness in our community.

The officers and trustees believes that the list of infraction and offenses constitutes just cause for disciplinary action and corresponding penalties imposed range from warning and reprimand to suspension and dismissal.

There maybe other offenses major or minor which are not covered by the list, the main point is good performance and the ability to work with others.

The success, therefore of our operations both limited and cumulative will depend upon the fullest cooperation of all concerned. We believed that all of us can do our part easily and gladly.


I. WORK AND DRIVE EFFICIENCY

1. Any act of insubordination:

1st Offense – Suspension three (3) days

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

4th Offense – Dismissal

2. Illegal lining other than prescribed point:

1st Offense – Suspension three (3) days

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

4th Offense – Dismissal

3. Illegal pick-up of passengers:

1st Offense – Suspension three (3) days

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

4th Offense – Dismissal

4. Wearing sandos, short pants and sandals from Monday to Friday are not authorized, except when declared by the President during rainy days wearing of sandals and shorts pants are authorized:

1st Offense – Suspension three (3) days

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

4th Offense – Dismissal

5. Wearing sandos during Saturday, Sunday and Holiday are not authorized:

1st Offense – Suspension three (3) days

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

4th Offense – Dismissal

6. Wearing Slippers and short pants during weekdays:

1st Offense – Suspension three (3) days

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

4th Offense – Dismissal

II. ACT OF DISHONEST

7. Failure and or Refusing to pay the daily dues:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) day

2nd offense – Suspension three (3) days

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

4th Offense – Dismissal

8. FALSE STATEMENT – Any applicant who had been accepted for membership and later found out to have fraudulent entries in order to “influence” approval:

1st Offense – Dismissal

III. ACT AGAINST PUBLIC POLICY

9. Driving under the influence of liquor, drug and or participating in any drinking spree within the WEBTTODA premises or any form of illegal gambling within the route:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week

2nd offense – Dismissal

10. Defacing and or tearing down posters of the association from bulletin board, or adding insulting words and or pictures or marks:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) month

3rd Offense – Dismissal

11. Fighting in the association regardless of the cause:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week

2nd offense – Dismissal

12. Attacking another member without any provocation causing bodily harm/and or injury:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week

2nd offense – Dismissal

13. Challenging any member to fight:

1st Offense – Suspension two (2) weeks

2nd offense – Dismissal

14. Challenging Officers and Trustees:

1st Offense – Suspension two (2) weeks or dismissal

2nd offense – Dismissal

15. Discourteous Acts Committed against passengers within the association playing route area:

1st Offense – Suspension three (3) days

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

3rd Offense – Dismissal

16. Reckless driving within the WEBTTODA playing routes:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week or dismissal

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) week

17. Cause ill-will and dissension or create cliques and/or intrigues among the officers, trustees and members:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) month or dismissal

3rd Offense – Dismissal

18. Treating coercing and intimidating below members:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week

2nd offense – Suspension one (1) month

3rd Offense – Dismissal

IV. ACT AGAINST PUBLIC POLICY

19. Conviction of any crime where the penalty is for one (1) month or more:

1st Offense – Dismissal

20. Making malicious false accusation and statement concerning the good name of the association:

1st Offense – Dismissal

21. Substituting old parts for operators owned property and appropriating same:

1st Offense – Dismissal

22. Misusing, destroying, defacing and or damaging association property:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) week or Dismissal

23. Acts of disrespect or discourtesy to the association executive:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) month

2nd offense – Dismissal

24. Insulting and or unbecoming conduct and or language to the association officers and trustees:

1st Offense – Suspension two (2) weeks

2nd offense – Dismissal

25. Disobedience to lawful orders of Marshall:

1st Offense – Suspension two (2) weeks or dismissal

2nd offense – Dismissal

26. Failure to attend the general meeting during the designated time and place especially drivers without valid reason:

1st Offense – Suspension one (1) day

2nd offense – Suspension three (3) days

3rd Offense – Dismissal

27. Failure to observe personal cleanliness, uncouth clothings:

1st Offense – Warning

2nd offense – Suspension three (3) days

3rd Offense – Suspension one (1) week

4th Offense – Dismissal

V. REPEATED VIOLATIONS

28. Three warning within a period of one (1) year last violation:

Suspension – one (1) week

29. Three suspension within a period of one (1) year last violation:

Dismissal

30. If the offense only calls for a lighter penalty such as warning and or/suspension, but the offense itself results in serious and or total injury to any one of the management shall order the immediate dismissal of the erring members.

VI. MEMBERSHIP/DISMEMBERSHIP

31. Operators with tricycle unit and active drivers are the bona fide members of the WEBTTODA Association.

32. Operators as non drivers after waiving the rights to operate within the WEBTTODA line or sold his/her tricycle unit to anybody will be automatically dismember/termination of benefits with the association.

33. Operators as drivers after waiving the rights to operate or sold their units to others will maintain his membership as driver, provided that he will drive once within a period of three (3) months. Non compliance with the above will automatically be dismembered.

34. Active driver will maintain his membership provided that he will drive once within a period of three (3) months within the WEBTTODA line. Non compliance with the above will automatically be dismembered.

35. Dismembered or terminated driver who re-applies for his membership shall accomplish the requirement as a new applicant and shall pay the membership fee amounting to 50% of the current driver’s membership fee.

36. Operators with tricycle units whose intention is to sell his/her unit or waive his/her rights to operate shall have clearance from the President of the Association to maintain the association’s ruling that, the first priority are the bona fide members of the association before the non-members. He/she shall accomplish transfer of rights or ownership for the new operator. Transfer of rights for non-members is ten thousand pesos (Php. 10,000.00) whereas a current member is three thousand pesos (Php. 3,000.00).


37. Old member facilitating the sale of tricycle unit without the knowledge of the association to evade membership fee of the new owner is subject to board hearing and general member for dismissal.



APPROVED FOR DISSEMINATION
